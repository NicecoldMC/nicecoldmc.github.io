Photosensitivity warning

This pack includes Natural Motion Blur. Fast movement with motion blur can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If that could affect you, disable or remove the mod before you play.

Install in a minute

STEP 1

Get the Modrinth App

Download it from modrinth.com/app, or use any launcher that imports .mrpack files, such as Prism.

STEP 2

Install the pack

Open the Null PvP+ page and click Install. In another launcher, grab the .mrpack from the Versions tab and import it.

STEP 3

Launch and play

Start the profile. The first launch can take a little longer while everything loads. Giving Minecraft 4 GB of RAM is a good starting point.

Questions

Will this get me banned on servers?

The pack has no mods that give you a combat advantage over other players. Servers still set their own mod rules, so check yours before joining.

Which Minecraft versions are supported?

Version 2.0.0 is for Minecraft 26.2 on Fabric. Other versions are listed on the Versions tab of the Modrinth page.

Can I remove mods I do not want?

Yes. Disable them in the content tab of the Modrinth App. Keep the libraries, because other mods depend on them.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub and include your Minecraft version and what you were doing when it happened.
